Tennis Games Scorer

Score tennis games, drills and kids' games from the court.

What it does

Tennis Games Scorer keeps score for more than 45 tennis games and drills — 100-Point Tennis, traditional matches (including short sets), pickleball scoring, King of the Court, Rush and Crush, Hold the Net, Dingles, relays, timed indoor drills and kids' games — with the rules for each, a game clock, rosters and player rankings.
