Privacy Policy

Tennis Games Scorer · effective 3 October 2026

The short version

Tennis Games Scorer does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your phone

Anything you enter — player names, rosters, rankings, game scores, results and settings — is saved only on your device, so a game survives the app closing. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Names are optional: you can play with team names only.

Deleting the app deletes all of this data.

Children

The app is meant for coaches, parents and players to keep score. It does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
